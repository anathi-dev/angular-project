About Ketshengana Hair Studio

Discover our story, mission, and the talented team behind the artistry

Our Story

Ketshengana Hair Studio was established in 2025 by a passionate hairstylist: to create a premium, safe, afforadble and stylish environment for hair care in Pretoria.

What started as a small, referral-based operation grew into a bustling salon, renowned for expertise in intricate braids, natural hair care any many more.

Today, we continue to honour our roots while embracing innovation, ensuring every client leaves feeling confident and beautiful.

Hair by Nathi - Your trusted stylist for all your hair needs.

Our Mission

To provide high-quality, innovative, and personalized hair styling services that empower our clients to express their unique identity and feel confident.

Our Vision

To become the leading hair studio in Gauteng, recognised for creativity, professionalism, and use of premium hair products, while fostering a community of hair enthusiasts.
