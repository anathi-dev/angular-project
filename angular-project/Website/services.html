Service Descriptions

Free Hand Styles

Creative and unique freehand braiding styles including cornrows, straight up, and fishbone cornrows. Perfect for those who want a custom, artistic look.

Knotless Braids

Gentle on the scalp with a natural-looking finish. Available in small, medium, large, and X-large sizes. Low tension and comfortable to wear.

Normal Braids

Classic traditional braiding styles that are timeless and versatile. Great for protective styling and low maintenance.

Kiddies Styles

Fun and age-appropriate styles for children using their own hair. Includes straight back, straight up, and creative pattern styles.

House Calls

Convenient at-home hair services. Available from R60 depending on your location. Perfect for those who prefer styling in the comfort of their own home.
